Support

Contact us

For bug reports, feedback, or any question about EuchreLab, email us at verselabapp@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play with friends?

From the home screen, choose Create Private Game to get a shareable game code, or Join Private Game to enter a friend's code. You can also use Multiplayer Lobby to be matched with other players.

Can I play offline?

Yes — Quick Play lets you play against AI opponents without an internet connection or an account.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest at any time. Creating an account (via email, Google, or Apple) lets you save your game history and stats across devices and games.

Does EuchreLab show ads during gameplay?

No. Ads only appear between games and on non-gameplay screens, never while a hand is in progress.

I found a bug or a rules issue — how do I report it?

Please email verselabapp@gmail.com with a description of what happened and, if possible, which screen you were on and whether you were playing solo or multiplayer.

Delete My Account

You can request deletion of your EuchreLab account and all associated data (email, display name, avatar, game history, and statistics) at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

To request deletion: email verselabapp@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my EuchreLab account." If you signed up with Google or Apple and don't recall which email that is, include your in-app display name so we can locate your account.

We will delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days of receiving your request. Locally-stored guest data can be erased at any time simply by uninstalling the app, since it never leaves your device.
