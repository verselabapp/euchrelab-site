Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Verse Labs, LLC ("we," "us," or "Verse Labs") operates EuchreLab (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using EuchreLab, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

If you create an account (via email & password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple), we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A display name (chosen by you, or provided by Google/Apple)
  • A profile avatar, if you choose to upload one
  • An internal account identifier and the sign-in method used

1.2 Guest play

You can also play EuchreLab as a guest without creating an account. Guest identities are randomly generated and stored only on your device — we do not collect your email, name, or any other personal information for guest play. Guest game results and statistics are saved locally on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers. If a guest plays an online multiplayer game, gameplay data (moves, scores, and the guest's random device identifier) is temporarily transmitted through our backend to synchronize the live game with other players, but it is not linked to a real identity and is not retained as part of any account history.

1.3 Gameplay & statistics

For registered accounts, we store game history, win/loss records, and ranking/statistics so you can track your progress across sessions and devices.

1.4 Multiplayer game data

Euchre is a multiplayer game. During a live multiplayer game, your display name, avatar, and in-game moves are visible to the other players seated at your table, as is necessary for the game to function.

1.5 Advertising data

EuchreLab uses Google AdMob to show ads between games and in non-gameplay screens (we do not show ads during active gameplay). AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers and other information to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage ad personalization through your device's advertising settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and maintain your account and game history
  • To synchronize live multiplayer games between players
  • To display your chosen display name and avatar to other players in your games
  • To display advertising that helps keep EuchreLab free
  • To diagnose and fix bugs, and to keep the App secure

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

EuchreLab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention

We retain account information and game history for as long as your account is active. Guest data never leaves your device except transiently during a live multiplayer game, and is not retained by us afterward.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access or correct your information from the Profile screen in the App.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time — see Delete My Account.
  • Opt out of ad personalization using your device's advertising settings.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including row-level security on our database, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Effective date" above when we do.
